What Every Element Is Intended To Do
Every control mapped to its intended behaviour — with open questions left for the team to answer.

Purpose

ANCRA is a design prototype. This document maps what the designer placed on each screen to the behaviour a developer must build on our own infrastructure. It does not report the prototype's controls as broken — that is expected and is the reason this specification exists. Where the design leaves a question open, the document leaves space for the team to answer it rather than guessing.
